I am an NLP Scientist at the National Board of Medical Examiners ([NBME](https://www.nbme.org/research/meet-our-experts)), where I focus on the intersection of AI/NLP and medical assessment. In this role, I contribute to research on automated scoring, aiming to enhance the efficiency of standardized testing methods, and I explore how NLP can improve patient-provider communication scoring and item generation.

Prior to joining NBME, I completed my PhD in Computer Science at the University of Georgia under the supervision of Professor [Sheng Li](https://sheng-li.org/). During my doctoral studies and multiple internships (Adobe, Reuters, etc.), my research centered on enhancing machine comprehension of data by integrating auxiliary knowledge.

Outside of professional activities, I enjoy playing the setar and capturing moments through street photography.
